DM TOOLKIT: REMAINING WORK
==========================
Updated 2026-10-17. Phases 0-5B are implemented in the current worktree.

1. PHASES 0-5B: CORE APP (IMPLEMENTED)
-------------------------------------
[x] Map editor, layers, tools, undo/redo, autosave and keyboard shortcuts.
[x] Asset library, user tilesets, procedural map generation and exports.
[x] DM Board: manual combat tracking, conditions, notes, rules search and quick tools.
[x] Combat Mode: linked tokens, movement, templates, batch damage, turn
    reminders, concentration prompts, manual turn notes and undoable combat log.
    Dice are rolled outside the toolkit.

2. PHASE 6: DM CAMPAIGN WORKFLOW (IMPLEMENTED)
----------------------------------------------
[x] Encounter library: save and reuse combat rosters; associate encounters with
    saved maps; start an encounter with its token links and initiative setup.
[x] NPC and creature templates: save reusable creature stats and token defaults;
    quickly add a template to initiative or place it on a map.
[x] Session log: record dates, recaps, decisions and open plot threads; link
    entries to notes, NPCs, encounters and map rooms.
[x] Party and quest tracker: maintain lightweight party-member and objective
    records, and link them from session notes.
[x] Character roster: keep player/class/level and combat-relevant stats; add a
    saved character directly to initiative.
[x] Manual combat mode: let the DM enter battle details and initiative, track
    turns and actions, and prominently show who is next.
[x] Keep dice rolls outside the program; manually enter initiative, damage,
    and save outcomes instead of rolling in the combat UI.
[x] Rest and recovery checklist: let the DM choose which tracked resources,
    conditions and effects to reset for a short or long rest; keep edition/rule
    assumptions visible and changes undoable.
[x] Treasure and loot notes: track items, holders and where they were found;
    connect entries to sessions and encounters.
[x] Keep campaign data local in IndexedDB and provide JSON export/import before
    considering any synchronization service.
